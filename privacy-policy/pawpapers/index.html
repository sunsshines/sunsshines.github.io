Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Your privacy is important to Team Wellplay. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PawPapers Android application (the "Service").

In this Privacy Policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to Team Wellplay. If you have questions, requests, or complaints about this policy, contact us at wellplay.id@gmail.com.

PawPapers is provided as an advertising-supported app. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other profile information to use the app.

Information Stored on Your Device

PawPapers stores certain information locally on your device so the app can remember your choices and provide its features. This may include:

  • Favorite wallpaper identifiers and wallpaper details.
  • Downloaded wallpaper records and local file locations.
  • Language, theme, wallpaper quality, notification, and auto-save preferences.
  • The expiration time of an optional rewarded ad-free session.
  • Recent wallpaper search terms.

This local information is not uploaded to a Team Wellplay account or stored on a Team Wellplay server. You can remove it by clearing the app's data, deleting downloaded wallpapers, or uninstalling PawPapers.

Information Processed by Third-Party Services

PawPapers uses third-party services that may automatically process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Google Mobile Ads

PawPapers uses Google AdMob to display banner, native, interstitial, app-open, and rewarded advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App launches, taps, ad views, and other product interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers where applicable.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Users may reset or delete their Android advertising ID through their device settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use AdMob.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

PawPapers uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to provide optional app updates and daily wallpaper notifications. Firebase may process app version, device metadata, Firebase installation information, and a messaging registration token needed to deliver notifications.

Notifications are optional. You can disable general notifications or daily reminders in PawPapers or through Android settings.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.

Wallpaper and Image Providers

PawPapers retrieves wallpaper content from external providers, including Pixabay, Unsplash, and the Dog CEO API. When the app connects to these services, they may receive standard network and technical information such as your IP address, request details, and device or browser user-agent information. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

  • Pixabay Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash Privacy Policy

Permissions

PawPapers may use the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access: used to load wallpaper content, advertisements, and notification services.
  • Set wallpaper: used only when you choose to apply an image to your home screen, lock screen, or both.
  • Notifications: used only if you allow optional app updates or daily wallpaper reminders.

PawPapers does not change your wallpaper without your action. It does not request access to your contacts, messages, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Information You Send to Us

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive your email address, name, message, device information, screenshots, or diagnostic details that you choose to provide. We use this information only to respond to your request, provide support, investigate problems, and improve PawPapers.

We do not sell the personal information you send to us or use it for unrelated direct marketing.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall PawPapers. Downloaded images may also remain in your device gallery until you delete them.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of information you directly provided to us by emailing wellplay.id@gmail.com.

Information processed by Google, Firebase, Pixabay, Unsplash, or other providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion practices.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards and rely on encrypted network connections provided by our service providers where supported. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through PawPapers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent personal information directly to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PawPapers, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact: wellplay.id@gmail.com.
