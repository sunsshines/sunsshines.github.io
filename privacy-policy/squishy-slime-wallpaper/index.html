Privacy Policy for Squishy Slime Wallpaper

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Squishy Slime Wallpaper is provided by Well Play. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account, login, name, email address, or other profile information. Wallpaper choices, favorites, animation settings, touch sensitivity, speed, effect choices, and battery-saver preferences are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. Well Play does not receive this local preference data.

Live wallpaper

The app provides an Android live wallpaper that reacts to touch and renders animation on your device. Touch input is used only to create the visual interaction and is not stored or sent to Well Play.

Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display app open, banner, interstitial, and native advertisements. The SDK may automatically collect and share an IP address, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

You can reset or delete the Android Advertising ID in Android settings. Your device and regional consent controls may affect ad personalization. For details, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Network access

The app uses internet and network-state access to initialize and load advertisements. The wallpaper and preferences do not require a Well Play server.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's policies and retention practices.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
