Privacy Policy for Cozy Nature Live Wallpaper

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Last updated: October 29, 2026

Cozy Nature Live Wallpaper is provided by Well Play. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Android app, including its wallpaper, icon-shortcut, widget, and advertising features.

Android package: com.teamwellplay.cozynaturelivewallpaper

1. Information used on your device

No account or login is required. The app stores favorites, browsing preferences, wallpaper and motion settings, onboarding completion, icon-shortcut choices, widget themes, and theme-setup progress on your device. These preferences are not uploaded to a Well Play server by the app.

The wallpaper videos and preview images are bundled with the app. Playback does not require downloading your selected wallpaper or sending your wallpaper choices to us.

To let you choose an app for an icon shortcut, the app reads the names and launch targets of apps that Android makes available to its launcher query. This information is used locally to create and manage your chosen shortcuts; the app does not send that list to Well Play or pass it to AdMob.

Clock and calendar widgets use the device's time and date, not your personal calendar events. The battery widget reads battery level and charging status locally. The current weather widget does not request location or fetch remote forecasts.

2. Intended audience, age settings, and younger users

The app is designed for teenagers and adults aged 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. Before advertising is initialized, an age-group screen offers Under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, and Prefer not to say, with no answer preselected. The app stores only whether to use protected or adult advertising settings, not your birth date or the exact age-group answer.

Users under 18 and users who choose not to disclose an age receive Google's child age treatment and under-age-of-consent handling. These settings restrict personalized advertising. All ad requests use Google's G content-rating limit. Interstitial ads are disabled for protected users, and App Open ads are disabled for everyone.

The app does not ask children to provide a name, email address, photo, or account. The protected/adult classification is used to configure Google's advertising and consent services. Child treatment does not mean that advertising involves no processing: technical data needed to provide ads, measure delivery, and prevent fraud may still be processed as described below.

Parents or guardians may contact us about the app's privacy practices or local data. Please do not send a child's birth date or other unnecessary personal information. Information held by Google is subject to Google's applicable privacy practices and request processes.

3. Advertising and consent services

The app uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banner and native ads may appear in the app; adult users may also see interstitial ads at suitable breaks.

Google's SDK can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, app/ad interactions, and performance or error data. Depending on age treatment, consent, device settings, and available services, it can also process advertising, app-set, or other device/account-related identifiers. These data support ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

This app removes the Android Advertising ID permission and Android AdServices ID, Topics, and Attribution permissions. It does not request precise device location. These restrictions do not eliminate all network or technical data used by Google.

UMP checks the consent requirements applicable to your session. Where a privacy form is required, the app requests it before eligible ad requests. An Ad privacy choices entry appears in Settings when Google's consent service requires that entry point. Its availability and the choices offered depend on your region and applicable age treatment.

For Google's handling of data, see Google's Privacy Policy, Google's advertising information, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

4. Device access and external links

Internet and network-state access support advertising and consent services. Android manages confirmation of live wallpaper changes, pinned shortcuts, and widgets. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, personal calendar, or location permissions, and does not ask you to upload photos or files.

Footage credits and license links can open external websites when you choose to follow them. Those websites, your browser, and any advertiser destination you choose to visit handle data under their own policies.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local settings remain until you change them, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes the saved protected/adult classification and onboarding preferences, so those screens can appear again. Home-screen items managed by your launcher may need to be removed separately through the launcher.

There is no app account or developer-hosted profile to delete. Removing local data does not automatically erase information previously processed by Google or another website. Their retention and deletion practices are governed by their own policies; we cannot promise a fixed retention period or directly delete their records.

You can remove favorites, change wallpaper and widget choices, and use Ad privacy choices when available. The bundled wallpaper content can play without an internet connection, although advertising and consent services require a connection.

If you email us, your email service and ours process the message, including your address and anything you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and retain it as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations.

6. Security

Android protects app-private local storage using its platform security controls. Google states that data sent by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date on this page. Where additional notice or consent is required, it must be provided before the relevant new processing begins.
